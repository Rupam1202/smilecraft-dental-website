✔Ceramic/tooth-coloured braces (less visible)
✔Clear aligners — discreet, removable, comfortable
✔Retainers to maintain post-treatment results
✔Growth monitoring for children

✔Premium titanium implant fixtures

✔Single-visit root canal for most cases
✔Rotary endodontics for precise, faster treatment
✔Digital X-ray monitoring throughout
✔Post & core buildup if needed
✔Crown placement to protect the treated tooth
